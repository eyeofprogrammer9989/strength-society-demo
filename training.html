Ways to train

Five ways to work out under one roof. Start with one, mix them as you grow.

01

Weight training

Racks, benches, free weights and strength machines on a spacious floor. Build strength and muscle at your own pace.

  • Racks and benches
  • Free weights
  • Strength machines
  • Lifting platform

02

CrossFit

Open turf and plyo boxes for functional, high-energy training. Move, lift and push with the people around you.

  • Open turf
  • Plyo boxes
  • Group energy

03

Personal training

One-to-one guidance from trainers who know their stuff. A plan built around your goal, with someone watching your form.

  • Plan for your goal
  • Form coaching

Yoga classes

Yoga sessions to build mobility, help you recover and balance out the heavy days.

  • Mobility
  • Recovery
  • Balance

Cycling

  • Ellipticals
  • Cardio
  • Warm-up
